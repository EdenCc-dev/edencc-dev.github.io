🛸 Another week of exciting milestones for the Cysic Network! We’ve made significant progress, from a productive in-person Sprint session to a major Testnet achievement that moves us closer to the next phase. This update also covers key product enhancements, upcoming mobile capabilities, and the launch of our community feedback form. Keep reading for this week’s full highlights and updates!

🌟 Last Week’s Highlight

We had an exciting in-person Sprint session last week with our amazing team! It was a great opportunity to collaborate face-to-face and push forward on key initiatives. Say hi to the team and stay tuned for more updates from our productive session! 👋

🔥 Testnet Achievement!

We’re almost at 27,000 verifiers and 565 provers — a huge milestone as we inch closer to our next phase! A massive thank you to everyone who’s been working hard and contributing to the success of the Testnet. Cysic to the moon!

🛠️ This Week’s Product Update:

We’re rolling out significant enhancements to the Cysic Network to improve rewards, task capacity, and overall user experience. These updates ensure a smoother and more rewarding journey for our community.

Here’s a detailed breakdown of the latest changes:

Key Updates:

  • Rewards have been increased from 50 CYS + 50 CGT to 100 CYS + 100 CGT.
  • Task volume has been increased by 50%, with accelerated distribution.
  • Faucet claim amount updated to 0.1.
  • Verifier slots expanded from 10 to 20 per task.
  • Verifier rewards now account for 20% of total task rewards, up from 10%.
  • Prover2 rule updated: If Prover2 does not submit results, their share is redistributed to Prover1 (formerly went to Prover0).
  • Known bugs have been fixed for a smoother experience.

Updated Reward Rules:

  • Platform (10%): 10 CYS + 10 CGT
  • Provers (70%): 70 CYS + 70 CGT
  • Prover0: 49 CYS + 49 CGT
  • Prover1: 10.5 CYS + 10.5 CGT
  • Prover2: 10.5 CYS + 10.5 CGT (redistributed to Prover1 if Prover2 doesn’t submit results)
  • Verifiers (20%): 20 CYS + 20 CGT, evenly split among up to 20 Verifiers.

Original announcement link: https://discord.com/channels/1127954093990760539/1224646528250155139/1319274665297973319

🛠️ Mobile Version for Verifier Program Demo Coming Soon!

Soon, you’ll be able to run a Cysic Network node directly from your mobile device, enabling decentralized participation and the growth of the whole network node runners.

To showcase this breakthrough, we’re preparing to launch a demo video that will walk you through the features and performance of the mobile node setup. Stay tuned for the reveal, we’re making nodes more accessible!

🎙️ Discord Bi-weekly AMA

Thanks again for yesterday’s fruitful community call! We got a lot of good questions and feedback from our Cysic community, especially about the Prover system. We discussed the points raised by the community in an internal meeting this morning, and we are actively working on improvements. Expect to see changes in the Prover system in next week’s update. Stay tuned!

We have bi-weekly Discord community calls on Thursday where our co-founder Leo answers your burning questions and provides updates on our team’s progress to make ZK the end game.

🤝Phase II Testnet Feedback Form is out:

We’re excited to hear your thoughts and improve the experience! Participants can now submit any issues or feedback through our dedicated feedback form. The team will review all submissions daily to ensure we continue to optimize the Testnet.

Feedback Form Link: https://forms.gle/vwDcamXgrZ4QCyPG6

✨ And that’s a wrap for this week, stay tuned as we keep building the Cysic Network and Cysic Hardware Chip for the future of ZK and blockchain!

About Cysic

We are accelerating Zero-Knowledge Proofs with Next-Gen Compute. Instant Proofs, Infinite Possibilities.

Cysic is home to the leading real-time ZK Proof Layer, featuring state-of-the-art hardware. Our mission is to hyperscale the ZK revolution by delivering Zero-Knowledge Proofs with unmatched speed, simplicity, and cost-efficiency. Cysic’s innovative product suite includes the Cysic Network, which decentralizes and streamlines proof generation and verification, powered by our ASICs, ZK Air and ZK Pro. ZK Air fuels lightweight, seamless client-side proof generation, while ZK Pro turbocharges high-performance server-side proof generation.

Our team is dedicated to pushing the boundaries of cryptographic efficiency, ensuring that ZK technology is accessible, secure, and ready to meet the demands of tomorrow’s decentralized world.
