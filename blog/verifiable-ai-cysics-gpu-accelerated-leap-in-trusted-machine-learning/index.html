Artificial intelligence is powering critical decisions in healthcare, finance, and more — but how do we trust that these AI models run correctly? Verifiable AI offers a solution, using cryptographic proofs (specifically zero-knowledge proofs) to mathematically guarantee an AI’s outputs are correct without revealing sensitive data. Historically, generating these proofs for large neural networks was painfully slow, but Cysic’s cutting-edge platform changes the game. By harnessing massive GPU parallelism and custom algorithms, currently Cysic delivers real-time verifiable AI inference for a bunch of useful AI models. In fact, verifying a large model’s computations can be extremely expensive without hardware acceleration — a challenge Cysic meets head-on with GPU-centric optimizations. Below, we break down how our technology makes trustworthy AI both fast and easy to use.

GPU-Parallel Sumcheck: Thousands of Threads, One Unified Proof

One cornerstone of our system is the sumcheck protocol — a cryptographic method used in zero-knowledge proofs to verify huge polynomial calculations efficiently. Cysic’s prover divides this traditionally heavy process into tiny tasks spread across thousands of CUDA threads running in parallel. By distributing the sumcheck computations (and the associated lookup table checks) over the GPU’s many cores, we keep every shader busy and eliminate bottlenecks. Research shows that launching on the order of 10,000 threads can fully saturate a modern GPU (e.g. 10,240 threads on an NVIDIA V100 with 5,120 cores) , ensuring maximum throughput. In our architecture, all those threads work in concert on different parts of the proof — continuously and without idling — so the sumcheck and lookup steps finish blazingly fast. The result is a prover that scales with GPU horsepower: as GPUs get more cores, our sumcheck protocol automatically gains speed by using them all. This massive parallelism translates to lower latency and higher proof-per-second performance, bringing real-time verifiable AI closer to reality.

Optimized Field Arithmetic: Maximizing GPU Efficiency

Another technical breakthrough is our optimized prime-field arithmetic on GPUs. Neural network proofs involve intensive math operations on large integers in a prime field (the math underpinning our cryptographic proofs). Instead of relying on generic GPU routines, Cysic developed custom CUDA kernels tailored for these finite-field calculations. The focus is on minimizing memory usage and maximizing arithmetic throughput on the GPU. Concretely, our kernels keep data close to the GPU’s ALUs (Arithmetic Logic Units) by leveraging fast on-chip memory (like registers and shared memory) and avoiding slow global memory wherever possible . For example, we pack frequently used values into GPU registers (which are the fastest storage) so that computations aren’t bottlenecked by memory fetches . We also coalesce memory accesses and use warp-level parallelism smartly, so dozens of threads fetch and process data in lockstep for efficiency . Altogether, these low-level optimizations mean the GPU spends more time crunching numbers and almost no time waiting on memory. Our prime field math kernels squeeze every drop of performance from the hardware, thus performing more operations per second and speeding up proof generation. The bottom line: Cysic’s verifiable AI runs fast because it treats GPU architecture not as an afterthought but as an integral part of the design, achieving superior utilization of GPU compute resources.

Seamless Framework Integration: Proofs at the Push of a Button

Speed isn’t the only highlight — we’ve made verifiable AI incredibly easy to use. Cysic’s platform offers out-of-the-box support for popular ML frameworks like PyTorch and TensorFlow. This means you can take any existing nn.Module (or TensorFlow model), wrap it in our provided VerifiableModule, and instantly get cryptographic proofs alongside every forward pass. There’s no need to rewrite your model or understand complex cryptography. Under the hood, our system automatically transforms your model’s computations into a verifiable format and invokes the GPU-accelerated prover to generate a proof in tandem with the model’s output. This seamless integration allows AI developers to continue working in familiar frameworks while gaining the added trust of verifiable results
