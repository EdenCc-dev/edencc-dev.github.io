Dear community, it’s been an incredible week here at Cysic, and we can’t wait to share all the amazing progress and updates with you. Let’s jump right in and celebrate everything that’s been happening together!

🛠️ This Week’s Product Update:

We’ve been working hard to make your experience even better for Cysic Testnet Phase II. Here’s what’s new in the latest version — designed with our amazing community in mind.

  1. Verifier and Prover Program Upgrades: Details on how to upgrade.

Highlights:

  • Improved Verifier Reward Mechanism: Now all 10 verifiers who receive and successfully complete tasks can earn rewards (previously only top 6).
  • Synchronization Feature: Improved task flow to prevent disruptions.
  • Performance Optimization: Enhancements for stability and efficiency.

2. Website Banner Announcements: We’ve made an update to our purple website banner. From now on, we’ll be using this banner as an announcement board, so stay tuned for important updates and news!

3. Referral Link Usage Update: For better user experience, we’ve changed the terms around Cysic Testnet referral links. 👀

⚒️ Testnet Phase II Following Upgrades

Get ready, Cysors! We have a lot more exciting updates in the pipeline, stay tuned for these impactful updates!

  1. More Tasks Incoming: Additional tasks launching next week as we scale! From then on, more verifiers will be able to receive and complete the tasks.
  2. Verifier Reward Mechanism Boost: Rewarding 20 verifiers for completed tasks instead of 10 verifiers. This means more verifiers will be rewarded.
  3. Mobile Verifier Launch: Soon we’ll be launching our mobile version for verifier program! This is the dream of client side verification on your phone comes true.

🎙Discord Bi-Weekly AMA

💙️ Every two weeks, on Thursday, we host community calls in our discord. We had a great time hosting our AMA community call this Thursday! We chatted about Testnet Phase II, answered your questions, and shared some exciting milestones coming up.

Join Testnet Phase II with just 2 steps along with other Cysors! Grab an invite code in our discord community channel and start running your node using our verifier program tutorial or prover program tutorial;

🤝 Cysic and Polyhedra Collaboration Gains Media Attention

Last week, we proudly announced our partnership with Polyhedra to advance real-time ZK hardware proving. This collaboration, combining Polyhedra’s Generalized Kilby Reduction proof backend with Cysic’s cutting-edge ZK ASIC chip, is paving the way for faster and more efficient zero-knowledge proof systems.

For more details, check out our announcement on Medium.

The news has sparked significant media coverage, with features in: Blockchain Reporter, CryptoNews.net, Coinstats, and Gate.io. The article written by reporter Umair Younas highlighted the collaboration’s innovative approach, stating: “The project takes into account the combination of several acceleration approaches like the Generalized Kilby Reduction proof backend of Polyhedra and the ZK ASIC chip of Cysic.”

Stay tuned as we continue to make waves in the zero-knowledge ecosystem!

🐣 Community Role Nomination

These promotions celebrates our cysors’ valuable contributions and come with exclusive perks, making them true badges of honor in the Cysic universe. Check out the original award announcement to learn more about their contributions and what earned them these promotions.

About Cysic

We are accelerating Zero-Knowledge Proofs with Next-Gen Compute. Instant Proofs, Infinite Possibilities.

Cysic is home to the leading real-time ZK Proof Layer, featuring state-of-the-art hardware. Our mission is to hyperscale the ZK revolution by delivering Zero-Knowledge Proofs with unmatched speed, simplicity, and cost-efficiency. Cysic’s innovative product suite includes the Cysic Network, which decentralizes and streamlines proof generation and verification, powered by our ASICs, ZK Air and ZK Pro. ZK Air fuels lightweight, seamless client-side proof generation, while ZK Pro turbocharges high-performance server-side proof generation.

Our team is dedicated to pushing the boundaries of cryptographic efficiency, ensuring that ZK technology is accessible, secure, and ready to meet the demands of tomorrow’s decentralized world.

For more information, welcome to join the Cysic Community!
