Introducing Cysic Network

Cysic Network is a decentralized prover/verifier network designed to revolutionize how zero-knowledge proofs are generated and verified. By distributing computational power across a diverse network of provers, we enable faster, more cost-effective, and reliable proof generation.

Today, we are thrilled to announce a significant milestone in achieving that vision: P2P has officially joined Cysic Network as a Prover.

This collaboration marks a new era for decentralized proof generation, reinforcing the importance of strong partnerships in building a thriving zero-knowledge ecosystem. By integrating P2P’s institutional-grade expertise and infrastructure, Cysic Network is taking another step toward making ZKPs more scalable, efficient, and accessible.

The Role of Provers in Cysic Network

In Cysic Network, Provers contribute computational power to efficiently generate proofs, ensuring scalability, security, and decentralization.

With P2P joining as a Prover, the network gains a robust infrastructure partner that enhances proof generation capabilities. This collaboration exemplifies the power of cooperation in advancing ZK technology.

Why This Collaboration Matters

Zero-knowledge proofs are fundamental to the future of privacy and scalability in blockchain and Web3 applications. Both Cysic and P2P share a common goal: driving the adoption of zero-knowledge technology by making it more accessible, scalable, and reliable. This partnership is a testament to what can be achieved when innovators collaborate to solve pressing blockchain challenges.

By joining forces, Cysic and P2P unlock several critical benefits:

  • Enhanced Scalability — With P2P’s robust computational power, the network can handle higher demand without compromising speed or reliability.
  • Decentralization — By diversifying the network’s geographical and infrastructural footprint, this partnership strengthens the resilience and security of Cysic Network.
  • Efficiency Gains — P2P’s infrastructure ensures that proof generation is not only faster but also more cost-effective, benefiting all participants in the ecosystem.

Looking Ahead

The addition of P2P to Cysic Network marks a major step in reshaping the zero-knowledge landscape. As Cysic Network continue to expand, we are not only growing our prover ecosystem but also broadening our collaborations with more projects to enhance the network’s overall capacity and reach.

We are eager to explore new opportunities that this collaboration brings to the broader ecosystem. Stay connected for more updates as we push the boundaries of what’s possible in zero-knowledge technology.

About P2P

P2P is a leading blockchain infrastructure provider recognized for its innovative solutions and commitment to advancing decentralized technologies. With a proven track record in supporting major networks and protocols, their decision to join Cysic Network validates the growing importance of decentralized ZKP infrastructure.

About Cysic

We are accelerating Zero-Knowledge Proofs with Next-Gen Compute. Instant Proofs, Infinite Possibilities.

Cysic is home to the leading real-time ZK Proof Layer, featuring state-of-the-art hardware. Our mission is to hyperscale the ZK revolution by delivering Zero-Knowledge Proofs with unmatched speed, simplicity, and cost-efficiency. Cysic’s innovative product suite includes the Cysic Network, which decentralizes and streamlines proof generation and verification, powered by our ASICs, ZK Air and ZK Pro. ZK Air fuels lightweight, seamless client-side proof generation, while ZK Pro turbocharges high-performance server-side proof generation.

Our team is dedicated to pushing the boundaries of cryptographic efficiency, ensuring that ZK technology is accessible, secure, and ready to meet the demands of tomorrow’s decentralized world.
