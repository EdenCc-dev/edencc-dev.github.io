It’s been another great week at Cysic! Our Testnet continues to thrive, with more provers, verifiers, and rewards being distributed. Along with this, we’ve launched the new Android Verifier App on Google Play, making it even easier for everyone to get involved.

Meanwhile, our team is gearing up for ETH Denver, where Shervin will be handing out exclusive rewards — don’t miss the chance to catch him!

In our community this week, we also had a productive Discord AMA, where we dove into key topics like ZK verification and what’s coming next in the testnet phases. Lastly, be sure to nominate yourself for Cysor roles in the Cysic community. With a big marketing campaign on the horizon, there’s plenty more exciting news to come! Let’s take a closer look at what’s happening. 👇

🔥 Testnet Achievement!

The Cysic Testnet is reaching new heights, thanks to the incredible efforts of our community. Here’s where we stand:

  • Provers: 3,530 accepted
  • Verifiers: 178,644 accepted
  • Tasks in Progress: 37
  • Total Rewards Distributed: 1,512,400 CYS + 1,512,400 CGT!!!

We’ve also hit 500,000+ wallets connected to the Cysic Testnet. That’s over half a million wallets!

These numbers reflect the growing strength of our network and the dedication of the Cysors pushing ZK acceleration forward. Next stop? The moon!

🏔️ ETH Denver

Shervin, our Head of BD, will be representing Cysic at ETH Denver! From February 21 — March 2, Shervin will be on the move, carrying exclusive drops for those who can track him down.

Here’s how you can claim one of Cysic’s newest rewards:

  • Stay tuned to the Cysic X account to find Shervin’s latest location
  • Come up to him and say hi!
  • Claim your reward

Don’t miss out on the fun!

⏬ Android Verification App Now Live on Google Play Store!

We’re excited to announce that the Android Verification App is now live on the Google Play Store! With this launch, users can now get our App directly from Google Play Store, and start verifying with one tap. To celebrate, we’re introducing the Android Verifier Register, where top 500 participants can earn 11 CYS and CGT as rewards! Register your Android App Verifier and participate in our special campaign following the below steps:

Download Cysic Verifier App on Google Play Store:
🔗: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cysic.plus&pli=1

How to register Android App Verifier:

  1. Connect your wallet: https://testnet.cysic.xyz/m/referral/invite. This wallet should be different from your PC verifier. We suggest you using a new wallet for connection.
  2. Enter your invite code. (Need an invite code? Grab one in our Discord.)
  3. Choose Android as your device.
  4. Return to the app you’ve already registered, import your wallet, and enter your private key.

About Cysic

We are accelerating Zero-Knowledge Proofs with Next-Gen Compute. Instant Proofs, Infinite Possibilities.

Cysic is home to the leading real-time ZK Proof Layer, featuring state-of-the-art hardware. Our mission is to hyperscale the ZK revolution by delivering Zero-Knowledge Proofs with unmatched speed, simplicity, and cost-efficiency. Cysic’s innovative product suite includes the Cysic Network, which decentralizes and streamlines proof generation and verification, powered by our ASICs, ZK Air and ZK Pro. ZK Air fuels lightweight, seamless client-side proof generation, while ZK Pro turbocharges high-performance server-side proof generation.

Our team is dedicated to pushing the boundaries of cryptographic efficiency, ensuring that ZK technology is accessible, secure, and ready to meet the demands of tomorrow’s decentralized world.

For more information, welcome to join the Cysic Community!
