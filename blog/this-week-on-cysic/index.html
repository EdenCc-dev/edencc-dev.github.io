Happy New Year, Cysors Fam! 🎉 Wishing you a prosperous and exciting 2025 ahead. Let’s kick off the year with this week’s updates from Cysic!

We’re also introducing a brand-new Cysic Knowledge Hub at the end of this weekly report — a feature you can look forward to in every future edition. Let’s dive in!

🔥 Testnet Achievement!

On this day of January 3rd 2025, we’re at 43,000+ Verifiers and nearing 1,000 Provers! Thank you Cysors for working hard on your tasks on our testnet. Don’t forget to invite your friends using your referral codes. You earn 15% of the Verifier/Prover rewards your friends make!

🛠️ Website Update Completed

The website update is now officially complete! 🎉 This update improves user experience by enhancing network stability and reducing latency. All services have been fully restored, and everything is back to normal — no additional action is needed on your part.

🛠️ Leaderboard Coming Soon！

Big news, the leaderboard is about to go live! 📈 Stay tuned for the official opening time next week on our website, you’ll be able to see how you rank among other Cysors! Get ready to track your progress and compete for even more rewards! 🏆

🛠️ Phase I Rewards Ready for Collection

We’re thrilled to announce that Phase I rewards will be ready for redemption next week as our testnet tokens, $CYS and $CGT, following the below rules:

Phase I Rewards Collection Rules:

  • Task Points: 1 point = 1 CYS + 1 CGT
  • Activity Points: 10 points = 1 CYS

👍 Did you know you can stake $CGT to earn $CYS as rewards? To maximize your rewards, simply stake your $CGT, or convert $CYS to $CGT and stake it to earn even more $CYS.
👍 Your previous points are securely stored in our database, acknowledging your invaluable contributions to Cysic Network Phase I.

Thank you for your participation, and stay tuned for more exciting phases ahead!

📖 Cysic Knowledge Hub

❓ What is our current Verifier and Prover Reward Rules?

  • Platform (10%): 10CYS + 10CGT
  • Provers (70%): 70CYS + 70CGT total
    Prover0: 49CYS + 49CGT
    Prover1: 10.5CYS + 10.5CGT
    Prover2: 10.5CYS + 10.5CGT
    And if Prover2 doesn’t submit results, their share (10.5CYS + 10.5CGT) goes to Prover1.
  • Verifiers (20%): 20CYS + 20CGT total, evenly split among all participating Verifiers (up to 20). Each Verifier receives (20/n) CYS + (20/n) CGT, where n is the number of Verifiers who submit results (n ≤ 20).
    Original announcement link: Discord Announcement

❓ What's the difference between testnet token $CYS and $CGT?

$CYS:
Cysic Network native token. Used for: transaction fees, block rewards, & other network-related activities. Ensures network liveliness & vitality via transaction fee mechanisms. Incentivizes participation of users in network activities.
$CGT:
Nontransferable governance token. Obtained by staking $CYS &completing computation tasks. Unstaking takes longer than staking.

About Cysic

We are accelerating Zero-Knowledge Proofs with Next-Gen Compute. Instant Proofs, Infinite Possibilities.

Cysic is home to the leading real-time ZK Proof Layer, featuring state-of-the-art hardware. Our mission is to hyperscale the ZK revolution by delivering Zero-Knowledge Proofs with unmatched speed, simplicity, and cost-efficiency. Cysic’s innovative product suite includes the Cysic Network, which decentralizes and streamlines proof generation and verification, powered by our ASICs, ZK Air and ZK Pro. ZK Air fuels lightweight, seamless client-side proof generation, while ZK Pro turbocharges high-performance server-side proof generation.

Our team is dedicated to pushing the boundaries of cryptographic efficiency, ensuring that ZK technology is accessible, secure, and ready to meet the demands of tomorrow’s decentralized world.
