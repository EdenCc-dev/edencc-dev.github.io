With the launch of Phase II, we now have 9,888 verifiers and 194 provers up and running! This milestone introduced the Proof of Compute Mechanism, the Dual Token Model, Staking and Delegating features, and the Referral Program launch!

⚒️ Testnet Phase II Following Upgrades

Our product and engineering teams worked tirelessly this past week to optimize the testnet based on community feedback. Here’s what’s been improved since the launch:

  • Verifier and Prover program upgraded for smooth access
  • Network performance optimization, the fluctuation delay is obviously reduced
  • Referral Program 2.0 launch
  • Search bar added to locate your address
  • New Verifier display: users can see that they are in active state
  • Maintenance banner is online, in the future after we start maintenance or any notification can be displayed on the webpage
  • Terminal Log tips description is more intuitive
  • The reward for the first 1,000 Verifiers and phase I WL were issued

❗️Groundbreaking Technical Report:

The Cysic C1 Chip Achieves Proving 1.3 Million Keccak Functions Per Second — 100x Faster Than Current Technology

This marks a monumental breakthrough for Ethereum and the future of blockchain. To achieve Vitalik’s vision of The Verge for Ethereum, fast proving speeds for hashes are essential. Until now, the best technology could only manage several thousand Keccak calls per second — a rate far from practical for The Verge’s ambitious goals.
Thanks to Cysic’s world-class hardware engineering team, the Cysic C1 chip now achieves proving 1.3 million Keccak functions per second , more than 100 times faster than the current state-of-the-art. This game-changing performance makes concepts like The Verge and Beam Chain significantly more achievable.
To validate this incredible speed, we used Polyhedra’s GKR proof system to measure the Keccak proving capabilities on the C1 chip, demonstrating its transformative potential.

About Cysic

We are accelerating Zero-Knowledge Proofs with Next-Gen Compute. Instant Proofs, Infinite Possibilities.

Cysic is home to the leading real-time ZK Proof Layer, featuring state-of-the-art hardware. Our mission is to hyperscale the ZK revolution by delivering Zero-Knowledge Proofs with unmatched speed, simplicity, and cost-efficiency. Cysic’s innovative product suite includes the Cysic Network, which decentralizes and streamlines proof generation and verification, powered by our ASICs, ZK Air and ZK Pro. ZK Air fuels lightweight, seamless client-side proof generation, while ZK Pro turbocharges high-performance server-side proof generation.

Our team is dedicated to pushing the boundaries of cryptographic efficiency, ensuring that ZK technology is accessible, secure, and ready to meet the demands of tomorrow’s decentralized world.
