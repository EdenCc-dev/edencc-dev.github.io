Cysic is Now Live on Succinct

Scaling Real-World ZK Infrastructure Together

We’re excited to announce that Cysic is now live on the Succinct Prover Network.

As a multi-node prover, Cysic is bringing high-performance GPU clusters online to support real-time, production-grade ZK workloads. This integration marks a significant step forward in making zero-knowledge proofs more scalable, accessible, and ready for real-world use.

A New Standard for Proving

Succinct Labs is building the backbone of decentralized ZK infrastructure with their open-source zkVM, SP1. Designed from the ground up for efficiency and modularity, SP1 allows developers to request proofs with minimal friction — and have them routed automatically to the most performant prover on the network.

Their vision is bold: to transform proof generation into a permissionless, decentralized, and optimized proving layer — one that enables developers to build without bottlenecks, while maintaining trustless guarantees.

At Cysic, we share that vision deeply.

Cysic: Proving at the Speed of Compute

Cysic joins the Succinct Network as a multi-node prover, powered by purpose-built infrastructure designed specifically for zero-knowledge workloads.

We’re not just operating compute — we’ve built the entire stack in-house, from the ground up:

  • Custom hardware tailored for ZK acceleration
  • High-throughput GPU clusters designed for parallel proving
  • A Layer 1 optimized for verifiability
  • And soon, our zkVM-compatible ASIC will go live, supporting SP1 natively

Our systems are engineered for one goal: throughput at scale. From hardware design to orchestration, everything is optimized to meet the demands of modern ZK applications.

Real-World ZK Needs Real Infrastructure

The age of ZK proofs is no longer theoretical. It’s happening — in privacy-preserving identity, age checks, AI audit trails, and verifiable data integrity. And with that shift comes a clear infrastructure demand: provers that can scale.

That’s where Cysic fits in. As one of the earliest teams focused on hardware-accelerated proving, we bring deep expertise in high-performance ZK workloads — including GPU, FPGA, and ASIC-based pipelines.

Whether it’s powering zkRollups or serving developers building new ZK-native applications, our role in the network is simple: deliver speed, reliability, and capacity at scale.

Joining Forces with Succinct

Together, we’re building a proving network that is fast, decentralized, and built to last. Plugging into the Succinct Network allows us to contribute our infrastructure and experience to a shared mission: making ZK practical and permissionless for everyone.

We’re proud to help lay the foundation for the future of zero-knowledge — one proof at a time.

What We’re Building Towards**:**

  • Scaling our GPU fleet to handle even heavier loads
  • Integrating CUDA pipelines for peak performance
  • Improving ASIC support for the Succinct prover
  • Expanding to support other major proof backends like GKR

Let’s build, run, and prove — together.

About Cysic

Cysic is an industry-leading ZK hardware acceleration project, dedicated to designing advanced ASIC chips to help reduce ZK proving generation time and become the first ZK Prover Network. It is a real-time ZK proof generation and verification layer, that aims to provide ZK proofs in the fastest, cheapest, simplest, and most decentralized way.

Cysic Provides ZK-CaaS (ZK Compute-as-a-Service) based on advanced self-developed ASIC chips and can undertake computing power requirements for all ZK computing scenarios including ZK Rollup, ZKML, ZK Bridge, etc. Based on chip computing power + community GPU computing power, it integrates many verification nodes in the community. Finally, it builds the Cysic Network to provide a credible integrated computing power + verification network for the entire industry.
