This week on Cysic — Jan 24th

Jan 24, 2025

This week at Cysic, we reached key milestones in our testnet, launched new community spaces, and rolled out exciting product updates. There’s also a new opportunity for Cysors to boost their rewards and stay engaged with our ongoing discussions.

🔥 Testnet Achievement!

The numbers are in, and our progress is incredible! We’ve passed 2,500 provers and we’re nearing 113,000 verifiers. Keep inviting your friends with your referral codes and links — you earn 15% of the Verifier/Prover Rewards your friends make. 👀

🏆 Leaderboard Launch

It’s been over a week since the Cysic Testnet Leaderboard opened, and the competition is heating up! 🔥

Cysors are climbing the ranks, tracking their progress, and earning rewards as they contribute to building the future of ZK hardware.

Have you checked your ranking? If not, there’s still time to jump in. Knock out some tasks, stack up points, and shoot for the top. Who’s taking the crown next? 👑

🛠️ This Week’s Product Update:

⭐ Phase I Rewards Collection

Cysors have been spending the last week collecting their well-earned Phase I rewards. With points from tasks and activities securely stored, many are staking CGTformoreCGT for more CGTformoreCYS and converting CYStoCYS to CYStoCGT to boost their rewards.

Have you claimed your rewards yet? If not, head over to the query page and make sure you’re all set for Airdrop Phase I! 🪂✨

⭐ More Tasks. More Rewards.

We’re excited to bring you more tasks in the coming weeks, which means higher chances to complete tasks and, of course, more rewards for your efforts. The more you participate, the more you can earn, so keep an eye out for new tasks and opportunities to level up.

⭐ Android Verification App Launch in Beta Testing Phase

Exciting news, Cysors! We’re thrilled to announce that the Mobile App Beta Testing Phase has officially begun. A select group of our community members with @Nova — Buzz roles have been invited to join this exclusive testing phase.

After the testing concludes, we’ll be rolling out the much-anticipated Mobile App Verifier, allowing you to verify your account directly from your phone. This update is designed to make the process easier and more convenient, so you can stay on top of things even while on the go. Stay tuned for this and more exciting updates!

👥 Community Update:

🐣 X Community Launch

We’re excited to launch Cysic HQ: your go-to hub for connecting with fellow Cysors, diving into ZK tech, and sharing ideas!

Whether you’re here to explore the latest in ZK hardware, ask questions, or post that meme you’ve been saving, this space is all about building together. 💬

📢 Get involved! Join now and make your first post. Introduce yourself, share your ZK journey, or drop something fun. Let’s grow this community, one post at a time.

🎙️ New Discord Channel Opened

We just launched #🦁｜text-bi-weekly-call on Discord! This channel is where we’ll drop the transcriptions of our Bi-Weekly Community Call sessions. If you missed the call or want to revisit the highlights, head to the channel anytime for the latest on team progress, tech updates, and all the exciting things happening at Cysic.

🎙️ Discord Bi-weekly AMA

Thank you to everyone who joined our community call this week! We had an engaging discussion, and Leo addressed key questions on topics like:

  • The potential for Cysic to support altcoin mining alongside ZK proof tasks
  • Updates on the Aleo prover, GPU utilization, and testnet progress
  • Plans to balance staking and task rewards in future phases
  • Hardware compatibility and the path to global ZK adoption

If these topics interest you, join our Discord channel to stay updated! We’ll be sharing a detailed summary of this week’s discussion there soon. Schedule our next Discord AMA.

🎉 Happy New Year, Cysors! 🎉

As we say goodbye to 2024 and welcome 2025, we want to take a moment to thank each and every one of you for your unwavering support and contributions throughout this incredible journey.

This year, we’ve made tremendous strides together, and as we continue to push the boundaries of ZK-powered mining, your dedication has been the driving force behind our success.

Let’s keep accelerating ZK compute power into the new year, achieving even greater milestones as a community. Here’s to another year of innovation, growth, and progress together! 🚀

Wishing you all a prosperous, joyful, and rewarding 2025. Let’s keep building a stronger future with Cysic!
