Announcing CyRunners Ambassador Program

Nov 3, 2025

To become a CyRunner,
1. Read Ambassador Program announcement
2. Fill out the CyRunners Application Form (link here)
3. Wait for our team to contact the selected ambassadors

📖 Overview

Cysic is building the backbone of ComputeFi — making the invisible machinery of the internet ownable, shareable, and profitable for everyone.

Today, we invite our most valuable community members to join us as CyRunners with the flagship Cysic Ambassador Program.

As a CyRunner, you’ll be part of an elite crew helping expand the network, spread the ComputeFi narrative, and shape the future of decentralized compute — all while earning rewards along the way.

💡 Why “CyRunners”?

CyRunners are Cysic’s frontline ambassadors: storytellers, builders, and advocates who embody the mission of turning compute into a new asset class.

Just like runners in cyberpunk networks, you help carry the signal across communities by running nodes, creating memes, creating content, and educating the next wave of users about ComputeFi.

CyRunners are the cultural messengers of Cysic. They’re running the ComputeFi narrative.

🧑‍🚀 Your Mission as a CyRunner

1. Amplify Cysic’s Message

  • Create and share content (threads, short videos, memes, infographics) spotlighting ComputeFi, DogeBox 1, and Cysic’s unique hardware advantage.
  • Help spread the story of compute as the new financial primitive: ownable, liquid, and yield-generating.

2. Community Engagement

  • Actively participate on X, Discord, and Telegram to answer questions, onboard new members, and keep the vibes going.
  • Spark conversations around ZK, AI, and compute culture to position Cysic as the leader in decentralized infrastructure.

3. Feedback & Insights

  • Report back on community sentiment, memes, and product ideas.
  • Share feedback that helps Cysic refine the roadmap and narrative.

4. Events

  • Organize local events in your town.
  • Build a local community and spread the ComputeFi narrative.

📜 Why It Matters

Not everyone is early as you, standing at the dawn of ComputeFi, with the foresight to see its potential. That’s why now it’s your turn to share your insights, knowledge, and bold vision for this new world.

Compute is already the world’s most scarce asset, fueling a frenzy that’s reshaping economies overnight. Everyone, from tech giants to startups, is racing to build massive data centers and compute hubs to meet the insatiable hunger for processing power.

NVIDIA, the king of AI chips, commands a staggering market cap of over $4.5 trillion, dwarfing the combined IPO valuations of entire waves of innovative companies in AI and cloud computing.

This isn’t hype; it’s the new reality, where compute scarcity drives trillion-dollar empires. You’re here early, positioned to educate and empower the masses, illuminating the path to a future where decentralized compute democratizes this wealth, making it accessible to all, not just the elite.

Help them grasp this: by joining Cysic and sharing this insight of the ComputeFi future, you are part of this movement.

👨‍🚀 Pick your role

(Emphasizing Influencers, Community Builders/Leaders, Local Organizers)

Applicants self-select based on strengths, with reviews ensuring focus. This attracts diverse talent, influencers for virality, builders for retention, organizers for grassroots growth, while tying to rewards (e.g., organizers get event grants).

Exclusive Rewards

By joining as a CyRunner, you will:

  • Earn Monthly Rewards
    → Base + bonus based on performance & creativity. Starting from 500$ base per month.
  • Gain Influence & Visibility
    → Access to private CyRunner channels with direct contact to the Cysic team.
    → Early access to campaigns, NFT drops, and ecosystem incentives.
    → Opportunity to co-create the narrative of ComputeFi alongside the core team.
  • Earn Recognition
    → Official CyRunner badge + roles, making your contributions visible to the community.

Why You Should Get Involved

  • Be a Pioneer
    → Shape a fast-growing project turning compute into a new asset class.
  • Grow Your Brand
    → Elevate your profile in crypto by being part of the ComputeFi movement.
  • Get Rewarded
    → Earn for what you already love doing — creating, engaging, and building community.

✍️ Application Flow

  1. OG Priority → First wave of applications is already open to Cysic Discord community.
  2. Form Submission → Share your interest, past contributions, and creative ideas.
  3. Cohort Selection → First CyRunner cohort announced within 1 week, 6 November.
  4. Onboarding → 2-day onboarding with:
  • Invite to private CyRunner channel in Discord.
  • Welcome call with the team.
  • Guidelines, badge, and first missions.

5. Activation → Get your first content prompts, meme challenges, and community interaction tasks.

👇 Next Steps

  • Complete the form → Tell us why you’re excited about Cysic & ComputeFi.
  • Stay tuned for the selection to join the first wave of CyRunners.
  • Be ready to amplify, engage, and earn.
